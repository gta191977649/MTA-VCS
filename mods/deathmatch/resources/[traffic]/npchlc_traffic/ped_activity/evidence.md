## Animation conversion

The importer reads seventeen original `ped`, `emp` and `gen` clips with the project's VCS bone mapping and 60 Hz key conversion. Each seat clip is four seconds. The table-cleaning clip is 1.333 seconds. The nineteen-clip package includes two derived beach entry clips that reverse the original rising motions. Losing the supporting prop, movement timing out, receiving damage, death and stopping the resource release reservations.
