# Police aircraft and watercraft

The US PS2 executable runs three different decisions for a vehicle: whether a unit is wanted, whether it can enter the world on this update, and what its current mission does. The helicopter manager runs separately from the traffic generator. The police boat uses the traffic generator and counts against its law-enforcement vehicle total. For moving road units, roadblocks and crew exits, see [ground response](ground-response.md).

### Tracking, searchlight and fire

The helicopter update at `002E64B8` processes these separately from flight steering. In mission `0x1B` the normal player-target branch skips player vehicles with raw class `+0x354` equal to 4 or 5. Otherwise `0024A350` supplies the occupied vehicle or the player ped. A vehicle flag at `+0xE8 & 0x10000000000` disables tracking and fire. The tracking history advances in 1,000 ms steps with a velocity lead. Strength is 1 below 40 horizontal units from the tracked point, fades to 0 at 60, and stays 0 beyond. Strength below 0.9 or horizontal tracking error squared above 49 restarts the acquisition timer. A sufficiently steady track can emit audio event `0x71` and schedule the next call for 4,500–8,595 ms later.

For a living pilot with a valid firing target, the acquisition delay in milliseconds is **10,000 / 5,000 / 3,500 / 2,000** at **three / four / five / six stars**. The switch assigns 999,999 for levels 0–2. World flag `004CD684 & 0x10` halves the selected delay. A target other than the player ped then replaces the delay with 5,000. These checks run in this order, so the on-foot six-star value does not apply unchanged to an occupied car. Wanted suppression bit `0x1000000000000` restarts both firing timers. When the acquisition deadline first passes, a failed visibility check restarts acquisition. After acquisition, a shot passes damage argument 20 to `002CE4A0` and plays audio event `0x37`. The hit routine passes that argument to the ped or vehicle damage handler when applicable. Its next-shot deadline is 400 ms for a random float below the ELF constant 0.15 at `00488198`, otherwise 150 ms. Both deadlines must be strictly past. [Evidence index](evidence.md)

### Officer deployment from the helicopter

The helicopter constructor at `002E62E8` gives the aircraft four deployment slots at `+0x851`. Mission `0x1B` with a remaining slot calls `002EA700` from `002E64B8`. Deployment requires a target, clear aircraft inhibition flag at `+0xE8`, resident model 9, and a random draw with its low seven bits zero. The target must be within 50 units in 3D and aircraft speed at most 0.1. At the selected attachment point, ground height must differ from target Z by less than 2.5, and a water hit must lie below that ground height. Failure leaves the slot available.

On successful rope registration the game creates ped type 6, subtype 3, stores the aircraft and rope identity on the ped, decrements the remaining count, and starts a 255-update attachment countdown. The four local anchor points use X = ±1.2, Y = ±1, Z = −0.5. On landing or loss of the aircraft link the ped switches to subtype 2. Model 9 is ordinarily requested by the four-star SWAT predicate, but the deployment routine itself checks residency. An already-loaded model or a script override can change the result at another level. [Officer landing behavior](officer-behavior.md)

During the countdown, `002E64B8` transforms each active door anchor into world coordinates and refreshes its type-1 rope through `00225C20`. The type table at `00486EA4` assigns length 20. The solver at `00226060` constrains each link to 20/32 units. The sampler at `00225ED0` multiplies the clamped progress parameter by 31 and interpolates between adjacent nodes. A fully extended strand therefore spans 19.375 units from its first to last node.

### Separate aircraft selector

The ELF also contains an aircraft selector at `003F8FF0`. Its wanted branch at `003F90C0` requires more than three stars, a current player vehicle of raw class 4 or 5, two model exclusions and a clock strictly beyond the previous timestamp + 60,000 ms. It counts vehicles on mission `0x21`. With none present, or with one present above four stars, it schedules signed model ID −983 for streaming. On the loaded-model branch, that ID selects mission `0x21`. No Ghidra caller, aligned direct branch/jump, or aligned pointer to this selector was found in the US ELF. In contrast, the Police Maverick manager has a direct world-update call at `003BAAEC`. The normal air-demand row counts the Maverick. An additional working interceptor in free roam is unverified.

## Predator

1. **Select the boat.** In `00430290`, a selected police vehicle class 13 or 24 on a node flagged `0x100` becomes model 219 (`0xDB`), class 23. The waterway switch must be on. If model 219 is unavailable, request it and return from this spawn attempt. The path must have a usable lane, a clear 40-unit spawn area and a valid water surface. See [waterway placement](../vcs-water-air-traffic.md).
2. **Assign the crew and mission.** Spawn sets style 2, clears the temporary maneuver, and seeds cruise speed with integer-truncated `12 + 4 × randomFloat`. The law-enforcement flag increments the shared vehicle count once. `0042ABB0` requests a driver only for this model. The driver factory selects type 6, subtype 0. `0042B1C0` assigns mission 4 at zero or one star and mission `0x15` at two through six.
3. **Update pursuit.** For a law-enforcement boat already in mission `0x15`, `00428C48 → 0042B670` writes cruise values **8, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40** for levels 0 through 6. The zero and one entries describe an already-running mission. In `0043A320` the desired heading points toward the player's position plus velocity times `60 × min(2, 0.05 × 3D distance)`. Positive speed error ramps throttle to full at one quarter of the cruise value. At or above cruise, throttle is −0.1, or −0.2 if the error is below −5.
4. **Use the mounted guns.** Mission `0x15` calls the boat firing routine only for model 219, 3D distance **< 40** and signed wrapped heading error **< 0.15**. The MIPS comparisons at `0043A620` and `0043A640` use the signed floats directly. The emitter at `002CF908` checks its own clock, uses two hull-local emission points, decrements the burst counter, and when it reaches zero resets it to 20 and advances the stored timestamp by 1,400 ms before the usual cooldown comparison. Each emitter passes damage argument 15 to the common shot routine. Separately, `0019FDA0` can play callout event `0x72` while a wanted player occupies a boat, in missions 2–5 or `0x15`, on its own 4,500–8,595 ms schedule.
5. **Pause or break off.** The tail of `00428C48` gives the law boat temporary maneuver 1 for 1,000 ms on the player-on-foot path or against handling classes 1 or 2. A boat target takes a different branch. `004380E0` handles the temporary control override. The pursuit update also calls `00428B70`: zero stars or the relevant suppression conditions clear pursuit style and select mission 1. The world flag tested by `0040D420` selects mission 0 on this release path. The separate `003EA0E0` flag setter decrements the fleet count when registration is actually cleared.

## Multiplayer implementation

The traffic server keeps one aircraft slot per eligible suspect. It samples the spawn ring through the suspect's client, bounds the accepted terrain reply to the original request, and advances an unstreamed aircraft until a syncer takes over. The syncer applies steering and obstacle avoidance. Other clients render the spotlight and ropes for streamed aircraft. The server owns target acquisition, shot deadlines, gun spread, deployments, and retirement. For each volley, the target client tests the issued rays against its local collision scene and reports hits on its own ped or occupied vehicle. An acknowledgement expires after one second and can apply damage once. Mounted guns currently damage the assigned target only. Callouts use San Andreas POL_ANN recordings for the corresponding boat and helicopter speech contexts.

An on-duty Predator uses the 400 ms emitter cooldown. The boat constructor at `0019C200` writes class 1 at `+0x354` for model 219. The emitter at `002CF908` selects 400 ms for that class when the duty flag is set and 150 ms otherwise. It normalizes the horizontal forward vector and emits two independently spread rays, each starting at its own muzzle. The mission's firing test in `0043A320` uses the player's current position from `0024A150`. The temporary brake in `004380E0` bypasses that mission for its duration. The port keeps the timer for one second after the target leaves the condition that caused braking.

Server response checks run every 100 ms and spawn eligibility checks every 500 ms. Timers execute on the first scheduler visit after their strict deadline. Rappelling uses a server-controlled descent and passes each landed officer to the ground roster. The SWAT selection predicate or a nearby streamed-model candidate represents model residency. Native vehicle simulation, collision results and network timing can change when the port reaches an original distance or speed threshold.

The MTA rappel uses a straight strand between the moving door and the surveyed landing point. The server advances the same armed model-9 ped along that strand at 0.003 progress per 20 ms time step. It releases the ped once the sampled position is less than two units above the surface. Loss of the aircraft or expiry of the 255-frame attachment releases the officer at the current position. The `ped/abseil` animation and collision suspension end at handoff. HLC then takes ownership of movement and the normal ground roster decides whether that officer can pursue the suspect. Each observing client creates one native SWAT rope at the deployment door. MTA simulates its individual segments and drops it when its hold time expires. Its API returns a boolean and accepts a fixed world origin. A moving aircraft can consequently separate from the visual anchor while the server still moves the officer. Late observers shorten the visual hold time using the distance already descended. [MTA native rope API](https://wiki.multitheftauto.com/wiki/CreateSWATRope)

Clearing wanted through the shared resource immediately removes that suspect’s officers still on the rope. A direct native wanted reset is also checked by the 100 ms server tick. If only the helicopter link ends while the suspect remains wanted, the officer unfreezes in place, regains collision and enters normal ground control. The independently simulated visual rope expires on its native timer.
