# Police officers, pursuit and arrest

This page follows the US PS2 executable `SLUS_215.90 (VCS)`. All addresses are virtual addresses in that program. The active player's wanted object starts at `player+0x900`. A field at `officer+0x920` is a different field from the star level at `player+0x920`. The register and state names below describe the observed reads and writes. The [pedestrian state index](../vcs-npc-dispatch.md) supplies the state-name crosswalk.

## Which decision runs this frame?

| Order | Condition | Result and continuation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Officer virtual slot `0x00490D10` enters `0x00324FC8` | Subtype 3 first runs its rope update. Every subtype then runs common ped control at `0x001F4BF0`. The common update can change the state seen by the remaining tests. |
| 2 | Stinger assignment `officer+0x92C != 0` | Run `0x00327D98` unless global mode `0x00489F7C == 4`, then return in either case. An already deployed strip in phase 2 also receives updates from the ordinary path at `0x00325070` outside mode 4. |
| 3 | Entity suppression bit, then ped state `+0x8C4` | Suppression returns immediately. Dead (0x3A) unregisters and clears the objective. Dying (0x39) returns. Arresting (0x36) delegates to `0x00327740` and returns. |
| 4 | Ordinary state | Cache 3D distance at `officer+0x904` to the occupied player vehicle or, on foot, the player ped. If the player is arrested, dying or dead and distance is strictly below 5, attempt arrest and return even if the attempt fails. |
| 5 | Officer is controllable | Subtypes 0–2 and 4–6 run duty logic `0x00325CD8`. The controllability test `0x001FD9E0` requires a state below 0x29, positive health and clear masked flags. A noncontrollable officer who is driving instead has a conditional vehicle-mission setup (bytes `+0x215=1, +0x214=0, +0x217=0x11`). |
| 6 | Controllable or driving (0x37) | Run the observed-vehicle checks at `0x00326E18`. The remaining state switch handles seeking an entity (7), seeking a position (6), idle (1) and attacking (0x10), including the arrest entries below. |
| 7 | Officer has cover/obstruction flags or repeated unsuccessful approaches | The tail at `0x00325958` handles the current target, tests cover geometry and updates the retry timer. For objective 8 and counter `+0x944 >= 2`, an obstruction query at officer Z + 0.65, radius 0.35, can unregister the officer, clear the objective, reset on-foot or driving behavior and defer enrollment for 30,000 ms. The alternate result can select an occupied weapon slot 3–6. The common ped update increments the counter at `0x001F67C8`. |

## Selecting whom to pursue

**An officer can cause the first star.** At `0x00326E18`, an occupied player vehicle of class 0, with halfword `vehicle+0x22E` other than 0 or 0xFFFF and entity status other than 5, triggers a minimum-one request when 3D squared distance is strictly below 400. A second path requires an unenrolled officer with objective 0x11 or 0x12, zero stars, a player-associated vehicle and bit 40 set in that vehicle's `+0x260` flags. Both call `0x002379E0 → 0x003AAC80`. The minimum-level helper can first restore the stored floor and then raise the result to one.

**Speech while enrolled.** Before the duty check, `0x00324FC8` excludes an arrested, dying or dead player. With exactly one enrolled officer, it requests event 0x83. Otherwise it counts nearby type-6 peds outside dead state. Below four, the officer requests 0x86. An on-foot player within 20 units can answer with 0x87 and look at the officer for a requested 1,000 ms. With four or more nearby cops, a random low nibble equal to 1 enables 0x81. These are requests to the speech system, which has its own bank and cooldown checks.

**Zero stars.** At `0x00325CD8`, a player ignore mask (`wanted+0x18` as a 64-bit value, mask `0x3000000000000`) can unregister immediately. Global flag `0x004CD684 & 0x10` has a distinct override that can switch an enrolled officer into stationary duty and unregister. Without these overrides, zero stars releases an enrolled officer unless the applicable aim-state branch (0x16) returns first. An available associated police vehicle can receive an enter-as-driver objective 0x12 when empty or an enter-as-passenger objective 0x11 when driven by a cop. A player at its wheel can trigger minimum wanted level 1. The vehicle predicate at `0x003EA160` compares a fixed list of model IDs.

On foot, an ambient officer with creation field `+0x8F4 == 1` and neither objective 8 nor 0xE scans its nearby-ped array in order. An ambient, controllable criminal type 18 or gang member whose relationship mask passes and who is not already fleeing a police ped receives objective 10 toward this officer. The officer receives objective 8 toward that NPC. A separate ambient interaction checks a controllable non-player in wander state 5, no objective or look target, elapsed timers, distance squared <25 and a clear line test. On success it requests speech 0x85, sets objectives 0xE and 2 on the pair and delays another interaction for 100,000 ms. Neither path inserts the NPC into the player's pursuit roster.

**Positive wanted level.** The ordinary branch reads the live count at `player+0x918`, the current allowance at `+0x919`, and officer pointers at `+0xC30`. It handles an unenrolled officer threatened by an approaching player vehicle before choosing who occupies a pursuit slot. For a non-melee weapon, the vehicle must be inside the current weapon range, horizontal speed must exceed 0.1 native units, and the normalized velocity dotted with the vehicle-to-officer direction must exceed 0.8. The officer can face the vehicle and request an attack before enrollment.

| Decision | PS2 behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Room in roster | Try enrollment at `0x00326FD8`. It requires `now >= officer+0x93C`, a controllable, unenrolled officer, and a clear current-activity flag (descriptor bit 34). It searches the first empty slot strictly below the allowance. A forced attempt bypasses the count test but still needs an empty physical slot. |
| Full roster | `0x003261A4–0x0032626C` compares cached distances across the larger of count and allowance. A candidate within 10 units can try for a slot. An officer beyond 10 can replace a strictly farther member. The eviction check protects an incumbent at or inside 10. Distances tied to the current maximum do not replace the previous candidate. |
| Allowance falls | An already enrolled officer runs the same scan when count exceeds allowance. Removal tests use the strict distance >10 guard. This is a per-update adjustment. |
| Enrollment succeeds | Increment count, record pointer, set `officer+0x908=1`, interrupt the current action, clear the previous objective and request objective 8 toward the player. The combat executor then controls approach, vehicle entry and weapon actions. [Combat decisions](../vcs-npc-combat.md) |
| Player is driving | A registered officer releases its `+0x914` contribution if present, decrementing `player+0x91B`. Release pursuit when cached distance multiplied by the magnitude of the player's 3D native velocity is **greater than 4**. Equality retains pursuit. The test uses `0x0024A238`. |

**Weapon changes after enrollment.** At exactly one star, an active weapon ID of zero and `player+0x514 == 0` select weapon 4 when owned, otherwise weapon 0. At levels 2–6, an active ID of zero selects 0x13. A selected specialist weapon survives this test. The standing roadblock branch separately restores the subtype's firearm at `0x00328200`. Re-evaluating the star level alone therefore cannot reconstruct the officer's weapon. The branch at `0x003262FC` also requests speech 0x70 when weapon 4 and officer flag `+0x914` are both active.

**Stationary roadblock officer.** `0x0040ADA0` writes `officer+0x90C=1` and stores the barrier vehicle at `+0x900`. With positive stars, `0x00325CD8` selects the firearm, faces the target, and checks its weapon range and action deadline. It can request a shot when a line test from the transformed weapon offset is clear or first hits the target. The branch requests timers with offsets 500–999 and 200–299 ms. During the alternate timer path, over 50 units a positive projection of target velocity onto the officer-to-target displacement releases the stationary role, tries enrollment and assigns objective 9. At intermediate distances, a horizontal dot product relative to the barrier vehicle distinguishes staying on the same side from releasing the position. Below 5 units the officer can leave the role immediately when the player is on foot or vehicle speed squared is below 0.000025. Attack states and the special `+0x920` flag can defer these transitions.

**Release.** `0x003271E8` operates on enrolled officers. It removes the first matching pointer, decrements the count, and fills the hole with the last subsequent occupied slot. It clears the objective and pursuit flags. A controllable officer with an associated vehicle and zero stars requests objective 0x12. With active stars and the associated vehicle strictly closer than 5 units, it switches to a close vehicle seek using 2.5. The fallback takes the player's XY, starts a 10,000 ms flee and sets movement state 2. Death, arrest target loss, obstruction and destruction also reach release. If the player is absent or this officer is already unenrolled, the routine has no effect.

## Arrest entry, maintenance and Busted

| Caller | Required state and strict comparison |
| --- | --- |
| General officer update, call `0x0032524C` | Player state 0x41, 0x39 or 0x3A and cached distance <5. No star-level check on this branch. |
| Seek entity, call `0x0032567C` | Seek step completes, objective 8. Player is already arrested and distance <2.5, or entering a vehicle (0x3D) or carjacking (0x3B) and distance <1.3. |
| Seek position, call `0x00325784` | The seek step completes, the blocking flag is clear, and the player is entering or carjacking but has no occupied vehicle yet. |
| Idle or attack, call `0x0032594C` | Objective 8, player entering or carjacking, distance <1.3. |
| Melee/non-melee combat, calls `0x001024D0`, `0x00100FD4` | Police actor, player target, target timer `+0x6C4 > now` or target being dragged from a car (0x3C). Ordinary working distance <1.5 in melee or <2 in non-melee. A fallen target with officer flag `+0x91C` permits <3.5. Officer fight state 0x11 selects <5 in non-melee. The distinct in-vehicle melee call at `0x00102998` checks the target's occupied vehicle and officer contact entity. |
| Vehicle / objective callbacks | Direct call sites `0x002FD390`, `0x002FD4DC`, `0x002FD5DC`, `0x003BFDD0` and `0x003EFEC0` can also enter arrest. The objective call at `0x003BFDD0` requires a police actor and its player target driving the checked vehicle. The seat resolver `0x003EF018` can return an officer/player pair when the candidate occupant is the wanted player and the officer's target pointer matches. Its callers then pass the pair to the arrest handler. |

1. **Accept.** `0x00327478` requires a controllable officer, a target and a clear descriptor bit 34. It requests target speech 0x6C and officer speech 0x6D. A vehicle entry/carjack can still fail after the speech requests if `officer+0x81C < now`. On acceptance that branch records the arresting officer at `player+0xC60` and sets `player+0x1C8` bit 62. The vehicle callback `0x002FD838` checks both, changes the player to arrested and clears the pending bit. Ordinary living targets enter 0x41 directly. Already arrested, dying and dead targets preserve their state.
2. **Hold.** The officer enters 0x36, clears its objective, faces the target and selects 0x13. Each update `0x00327740` retrieves the target. Predicate `0x001FDAE0` converts an ordinary living state back to 0x41. A missing target releases the roster slot. A target remaining outside 0x41/39/3A/3D/3B after the predicate, e.g. 0x3E, also releases it. Otherwise the officer ensures animation 0x97 is playing (or restarted if blending out) and aligns heading to the target. Shared occupied-vehicle handling can initiate the officer's exit. Changing an ordinary target state alone need not break the hold.
3. **Resolve the player outcome.** The normal player-info update `0x0024C428` sends 0x3A to the death path and 0x41 to `0x0024A770`. The latter requires player-info phase 0 and a clear external gate, sets phase 2, timestamps it, increments the arrest statistic and requests the Busted message for 5,000 ms. The wanted level is still available to the penalty calculation.
4. **Restart.** Phase 2 starts the black fade on crossing 2,048 ms. On crossing 4,096 ms it charges 100/200/400/600/900/1,500 at stars 1–6, floors the balance at zero, or consumes a one-shot fee waiver. It selects the arrest restart via `0x00371370` and runs `0x0024CF50 → 0x003AA730 → 0x003AA608`, which clears wanted state and roster pointers. The phase also clears the weapon inventory at `0x00200440` after the restart. At elapsed time ≥4,608 ms the player-info phase returns to 0. A dying/dead target attended by an officer consequently need not produce a Busted result.

## MTA officer and player interactions

The MTA pursuit worker checks the current weapon on each update. An ordinary officer who already holds the baton keeps it across a 1→2 transition. At one star an unarmed officer waits to choose the baton while the player has a vehicle or object underfoot. The server uses MTA's synchronized contact element for that test. [MTA contact element](https://wiki.multitheftauto.com/wiki/GetPedContactElement)

In the PS2 door-open callback `002FD098`, the virtual door update receives 1.0, and the occupant/officer resolver `003EF018` supplies the pair for `00327478`. At the end of the callback, a resolved player who equals the seated occupant takes the early return before the generic drag-out call `00300818`. The same early return appears at `003EFED0` after the arrest call at `003EFEC0`. The arrest routine sets officer state 0x36, faces the target and selects weapon 0x13. This path lets the driver remain seated while the officer holds them at gunpoint.

In MTA an enrolled officer using a firearm requests a burst while the driver is strictly farther than 3 units and strictly inside the active weapon's range. This includes a stationary driver. The server tracks the burst and recovery for each officer across movement tasks and sync owners. In recovery, at close range, or while carrying a melee weapon, a free officer can approach an unlocked, slow driver's vehicle. Roadblock guards have a separate stationary duty. The server sends the walking task a point 0.55 units outside the left collision bound and 20% of the forward bound ahead of the vehicle origin. The syncer approaches it around the vehicle, stops within 0.28 units, and faces the driver's window. The server checks the reach against the same local coordinates. The server checks the driver, roster, world, side, distance, lock and speed. The officer's syncer requests native entry into the occupied driver's seat. The server checks the ordinary entry event, including its veto, seat, driver and door. While the native task opens the front-left door, the syncer interrupts it as the hinge moves. MTA's jack-abort packet reports the actual angle to the server. The server checks the original syncer, driver, police assignment, lock, distance and hinge before custody begins. A missing or already-open door allows an immediate arrest. The officer selects the pistol if equipped and aims at the seated driver's upper torso until the station transfer. The reach, velocity and hinge thresholds are multiplayer implementation values. [MTA native entry](https://wiki.multitheftauto.com/wiki/SetPedEnterVehicle) · [MTA server vehicle handling](https://github.com/multitheftauto/mtasa-blue/blob/master/Server/mods/deathmatch/logic/CGame.cpp)

For pursuit speech, the extracted bank-2 selector returns 30 model-8 officer clips for 0x86 and eight player replies for 0x87. For the dispatched officer models it supplies no clips for the solitary 0x83 and crowded 0x81 requests. With multiple enrolled officers, the adapter samples nearby living officers in a 30-unit radius and caches the count for 500 ms per suspect. With fewer than four nearby officers, an on-foot target strictly within 20 units can answer. The original delay and jitter rows at `00485AA8` govern audible repeats. The MTA tests check the solitary, small-group and crowded branches.

### Prompt driver-door attempts

A pursuing officer already within the validated driver-door reach attempts the seated arrest before evaluating its firearm burst. Outside that reach, the existing shooting and approach decisions still apply. Failed door attempts retry after 250 ms. The client reports its first missed handle arrival after 250 ms, with subsequent reports still spaced 1,500 ms apart and capped at four reports. These are MTA responsiveness values, not recovered VCS constants; the normal door-opening animation and vehicle-speed threshold are unchanged.

The server revalidates pending attempts during pursuit updates and hinge confirmation. Lost sync ownership, an invalid driver, excessive speed, a lock, a changed pursuit target or an invalid reach cancels the attempt. Cancellation carries the attempt ID so a delayed cancellation or timeout cannot interrupt a newer reach. A successful opening still requires the native entry event and independently replicated hinge angle before custody begins.

## Specialist actions

**Helicopter descent.** `0x00227448` requires loaded ped model 9 and successful rope creation, creates type 6/subtype 3, records the rope ID at `+0x934`, sets `+0x930` and starts animation 0xA7. At `0x003279C8` the officer enters 0x29. The rope parameter advances by `timeStep × 0.003`. A living actor with a valid sampled rope position moves to that point. Ground less than two units below the officer, a cleared rope flag, an invalid sample, or nonpositive health finishes the descent branch. It restores the entity flag, zeroes movement, writes state 1 and changes the subtype to **2**. The same frame then enters common control. The stinger flag and this rope subtype are independent.

**Spike strip.** In population generation `0x002B17A8`, a wanted level above 2, global mode zero, a random result with its low five bits zero, and an occupied player vehicle set the stinger candidate. If the population and placement tests produce a police ped, `0x002B2588` sets `officer+0x92C`. Its subsequent deployment is governed by different conditions:

- `0x00327D98` requires an occupied player vehicle of class 0 or 6. Absence or another class calls pursuit release. With an inactive strip, level must be ≥1, horizontal distance <30, horizontal velocity nonzero, and normalized direction of motion dotted with the vehicle-to-officer direction >0.8. A valid nearby path node, valid officer path position, horizontal offset >1 and a nonzero side test determine which way the officer turns.
- `0x0039DF08` additionally requires fewer than 24 live segments, an on-foot controllable officer, an inactive strip and absence of animation 0x3A. It constructs twelve segments through `0x0039CF70`, enters officer state 0x42 and starts group 0, animation 0x3A. The US disc association names this clip `weapon_throwu`. The converted underhand throw has 22 skeletal tracks and spans 34 ticks at 60 Hz (0.567 s). The client plays it once when the strip is assigned age zero. On the tested MTA build, the animation getter reports `ped/run_wuzi` for custom IFP playback. Right-elbow flexion measured 15 degrees at 113 ms, 96 degrees at 402 ms and 62 degrees at 514 ms during a connected-client throw. The converted clip shows the same wind-up and release. [MTA custom animation reporting](https://github.com/multitheftauto/mtasa-blue/issues/3464) Each segment increments the global count at `0x0039CE9C` and decrements it at `0x0039CEE0`. Two intact strips fill the budget.
- `0x0039D340` adds the segments to the world once the officer is in state 0x42 and the animation progress field exceeds 0.39. Deployment uses the creation timestamp and advances to phase 2 after strictly more than 2,500 ms. Phase 2 clears the officer's assignment. Phase 3 is the reverse motion, and phase 4 removes the twelve objects via `0x0039D2C8`. Officer destruction also calls this cleanup.
- The segment constructor at `0x0039CDF0` reads the model index at offset `0xEE` in the GATG index table. In the US PS2 data this is model 621 with name hash `0xD5ED1691`, identifying `plc_stinger`. The MTA client uses its converted mesh and texture for all twelve slats.
- `0x0039D9A8` queries vehicles around the midpoint of the strip and compares their wheel positions to all twelve segments. It handles classes 0/9/10/11 and 6/8, requires the wheel's contact field <1 and tests squared distance strictly below `(vehicle wheel scale + 0.1)²`. The scale comes from the crossing vehicle's model-info field at `+0x58`. Matching wheels receive the vehicle virtual call at slot 0xB0. A vehicle other than the triggering player can hit the strip.

## Verification cases and evidence boundaries

In the ordinary PS2 restart path, `0024C428` requests a two-second fade to black on crossing 2,048 ms. The station restart occurs at 4,096 ms. The shared fade-in branch at `0024CC64` requests three seconds in the opposite direction. MTA runs that fade at 4,608 ms after every arrest and keeps the suspect frozen through the fade-in. It restores the gameplay controls that custody disabled. The timing of the input release is a multiplayer presentation choice. [MTA control names](https://wiki.multitheftauto.com/wiki/Control_names)

The officer's arrest animation is the converted original group-0 clip 0x97, `arrestgun`. The ordinary player state 0x41 has no dedicated animation call in the ped state dispatch at `001F8FB4`. MTA uses a held surrender pose for an on-foot suspect and retains the seated pose while a suspect occupies a vehicle.

**Busted camera.** The selector at `00374E90` recognizes arrested state 0x41. The camera dispatcher at `00294540` sends mode 0x20 to `0029A8F0`, which sets a 45-degree field of view and follows the suspect's upper torso. The entity-type test distinguishes a ped target (masked type 6) from a vehicle target (masked type 4). On foot it splits the officer-first and low-side-first choices evenly, with street fixtures as fallback. For an occupied vehicle, the normal selection tries a street fixture first with 65% probability, then the low side and officer views; the alternate ordering starts with the officer, then a street fixture and low side. The MTA adapter uses these same strict 0.5/0.65 probability boundaries.

- `002A40E0` places the camera three units to the side and five behind the arresting officer, at least eight units from its focus.
- `002A4538` uses five units forward and ten to the side. Its ground height is the surface plus 0.7, with a fallback five above the target. The shot can stay still, track sideways, or track sideways and rise.
- `002A4740` searches within 30 units for a visible, upright lamp, traffic signal or pole and favors a horizontal range of 17 units. The ten original model IDs come from the US `GAME.DTZ` table. MTA uses the corresponding map objects and raycasts against streamed geometry to keep the camera clear of obstructions.

If host collision leaves less than two units between the camera and suspect, the client checks alternative bearings at the arrest location. This prevents a rooftop edge or nearby obstruction from moving the camera inside the suspect during the shot.

| Walkthrough | Expected branch |
| --- | --- |
| One free slot, ordinary officer starts unarmed, player on foot at one star | Enroll, assign objective 8, choose the baton if owned and the player contact pointer is null. Moving directly to two stars with the baton already selected does not satisfy the zero-weapon predicate. |

The raw branch evidence verifies the states and thresholds above. Some shared activity flags have no confirmed descriptive names. The external Busted gate is the halfword at singleton `[0x0048F374]+0x21E`, returned by `0x0031DA80`. Its gameplay name is unresolved. A search of aligned PS2 text instructions for byte stores at displacement 0x23C found phase writes of 0, 1, 2 and 4 only. The phase-3 handler exists, but its activation through ordinary play is unverified. The vehicle callbacks also participate in the wider [vehicle interaction state machines](../vcs-npc-vehicles.md). The local San Andreas `CCopPed` constructor supplies comparison terms for models and equipment. All numeric tuning above comes from VCS.
