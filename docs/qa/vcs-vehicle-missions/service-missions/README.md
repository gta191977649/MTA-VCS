# Service missions, VCS radar and parked helicopter paint

One MTA 1.7 client connected to an isolated loopback server running the VCS map, model loader, traffic, frontend, marker and mradar resources. A temporary Lua resource positioned vehicles, observed the production mission events and saved the native 960 × 600 screenshots. The server granted private markers and attached blips to the test player. The client sampled the synchronized blip coordinates and recorded when the previous blip ceased to exist. Stationary objectives matched in XY. One sample on a moving fire victim differed by approximately 0.0034 m.

## Radar and vehicle changes

The client loaded all 64 PS2 terrain tiles. The VCS minimap was visible and the native San Andreas radar was disabled. Restarting frontend previously invalidated tiles cached by mradar and left the disk empty. The corrected radar reacquired the frontend textures and displayed the street map again. During a level-16 Firefighter dispatch the same run and three fire blips survived the restart and the timer resumed at 6:45. Earlier screenshots in the parent report cover the earlier HUD and rig work. [Radar following a frontend restart](restored-minimap.jpg)

The marker visibility check caught an old explicit per-player permission surviving after the player left a fire vehicle. After the fix, leaving the Fire Copter changed the visible count from 12 fire blips to one return blip. Re-entering restored the 12 unfinished fires. Cancelling each of the eight missions cleared its remaining synchronized blips and markers. The tests also compared the client effect and object counts and checked the existing Pay ’n’ Spray and bomb-shop provider registrations. Two simultaneous real clients were unavailable. Two-player concurrency coverage uses simulated participants.

## Cleanup checks

Cancelling a tour mid-hover removed its active camera-flash effect; a later sample found zero effects. Stopping the mission resource during a water drop removed the Fire Copter bucket, four nearby effects, 12 mission markers and their blips. Destroying the Air Rescue helicopter after loading a patient removed its sling, patient objective and hospital blip. In all three tests the mission had exclusive use of the frontend HUD before termination and another resource could acquire it immediately afterward. The five garage colshapes and both service registrations survived. The final overlapping garage run produced no client or server warnings or errors. The eight checked driver controls were enabled after cancellation, failure and resource stop. [Spraying before stop](spray-active.jpg) · [Same view after stop](spray-after-stop.jpg)

During a separate run the driver cancelled Taxi Driver inside garage 916 after the Pay ’n’ Spray had frozen the cab at 700 health. The mission released its HUD while the garage retained its service camera and completed the repair. It charged $100, restored the cab to 1,000 health and unfroze it. A second damaged-cab visit charged another $100 and repaired it again. On leaving the bay the garage camera returned to the driven vehicle. [Repaired taxi after leaving the garage](taxi-after-garage.jpg)

Disconnecting while an Air Rescue patient was attached removed the actual patient, rig, hospital marker and blip from the server with zero players online. Reconnecting showed no remaining objectives and restored the saved fireproof reward. On a final fresh connection the client measured wanted and parole at zero, all 64 radar tiles loaded, and the HUD drew normally. [Fresh connection](reconnect.jpg)

Full server shutdown exposed a frontend NPC health bar handler looking up an owner after its resource handle had expired. The handler now tracks the owner's root element. A second isolated shutdown with frontend running completed without the previous error.

## Parked Air Ambulances

The US MAIN script creates HUEYHOSP 189 with both colour arguments set to -1 at Downtown and Ocean Beach. The PS2 helicopter constructor at 0x002E62E8 and the model paint chooser at 0x003952B8 lead to the primary and secondary colour lookups at 0x00395E08 and 0x00395ED0. Both authored variations in the model record at GAME.DTZ 0x514260 use local palette indices 241 and 240. These resolve to white (255, 255, 255) and dark red (176, 0, 0). The fire station generator at MAIN.txt line 41017 also uses the default pair. HUEY 188 at GAME.DTZ offset 0x513FC0 uses the same two local indices, which resolve to white (255, 255, 255) and navy (36, 57, 100) in its own record.

Both actual parked helicopters showed that pair on the server and on the connected client while parked, after driver entry, after streaming out and back in, and after respawn. [Downtown](downtown-air-ambulance.jpg) · [Ocean Beach](ocean-beach-air-ambulance.jpg)
